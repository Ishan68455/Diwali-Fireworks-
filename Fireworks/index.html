Responsible Cracker Use & Safety Guidelines

Please celebrate Diwali safely and respect your community and environment.

Always burst crackers in open outdoor areas away from dry foliage or flammable objects.
Children must always light crackers under strict adult supervision.
Keep a bucket of water or sand nearby for instant emergency extinguishing.
Wear cotton clothing and avoid synthetic fabrics while lighting fireworks.
